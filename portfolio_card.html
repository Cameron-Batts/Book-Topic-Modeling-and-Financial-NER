Book Topic Modeling & Financial Entity Extraction

Problem
Large text collections need interpretable themes and reliable entity extraction to support browsing and document review.
Approach
Compared five- and ten-topic LDA models on 16,559 book summaries, varied training passes, and inspected spaCy entity extraction across 4,846 financial-news sentences.
Impact
Four LDA configurations and raw-text full-span NER were executed. Training-corpus fit and aggregate entity counts are exploratory; no held-out topic or NER accuracy was measured.
